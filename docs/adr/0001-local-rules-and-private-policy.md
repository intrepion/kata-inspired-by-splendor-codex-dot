# Local rules engine and restricted AI observations

Accepted 2026-10-22. The user authorized sensible design decisions without an interview.

A two-player, dependency-free game keeps the market readable and runs from both loopback and a direct file. Separate pure rules, AI observation/policy, and browser controller. All legal transitions go through one engine; the policy receives deck counts and only permitted reserve identities. This avoids a search engine accidentally evaluating private decks. The AI is a deterministic heuristic, not an optimal solver.

Use original, rotationally symmetric cost profiles with 40/30/20 plans and ten original patrons. This preserves the core economy and action rules without claiming the original card distribution or balance. Procedural vector architecture gives every plan an original image without downloads.

Keep local saved state for interrupted matches; no network game state, accounts or external services. Required resolution dialogs cannot be escaped, while voluntary inspection and restart can be canceled. AI callbacks are canceled by a generation counter on restart and wait while a modal is open.
