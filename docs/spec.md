# Prism Atelier specification

Build an original complete four-player browser game inspired by base Splendor. Human vs three deterministic fair AIs by default; optional two-/three-player tables and selectable first seat. Follow the publisher's current base-game action, stock, payment, reserve, token-return, noble, final-round and tie rules. Use an original title, art, card names and costs; do not imply identical card balance.

Provide 90 original plans (40/30/20), four visible per tier, ten possible patrons with five selected, seven tokens per color and five gold (four/three patrons and five/four tokens per color at three/two players). Reserve from market or blind deck, buy from market or own reserve. Display distinct opponent identities, holdings and clockwise turn order. Reveal only previously public opponent reserves. Expose no hidden information to the AI policy.

A turn consists of an action, required returns to ten, a mandatory eligible patron choice (at most one), then turn advancement. Gold substitution remains elective even when colored tokens are available. Final round gives equal turns; compare prestige, then fewest purchased plans, then share victory.

Provide clear onboarding and handbook, legal-action guidance, plan inspection, full holdings, public AI reserves, move suggestions, results, replay and restart confirmation. Support keyboard, mouse and touch, narrow displays, reload resume and interrupted/repeated actions across every AI seat. Preserve older two-player saves as two-player matches while making new matches default to four. Preserve unrelated work and servers. Serve loopback only, no paid assets or hosting.

Validate deterministic edge cases and complete matches, browser complete games and interruption flows, independent review and native acceptance. Publish authorized public GitHub repo with README, MIT and full official OS gitignores; commit/push and verify remote SHA and exact-commit CI. Leave durable detached preview.
