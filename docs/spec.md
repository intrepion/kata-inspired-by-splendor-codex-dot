# Prism Atelier specification

Build an original complete two-player browser game inspired by base Splendor. Human vs deterministic fair AI; selectable first player. Follow the publisher's current base-game action, stock, payment, reserve, token-return, noble, final-round and tie rules. Use an original title, art, card names and costs; do not imply identical card balance.

Provide 90 original plans (40/30/20), four visible per tier, ten possible patrons with three selected, four tokens per color and five gold. Reserve from market or blind deck, buy from market or own reserve. Reveal only previously public opponent reserves. Expose no hidden information to the AI policy.

A turn consists of an action, required returns to ten, a mandatory eligible patron choice (at most one), then turn advancement. Gold substitution remains elective even when colored tokens are available. Final round gives equal turns; compare prestige, then fewest purchased plans, then share victory.

Provide clear onboarding and handbook, legal-action guidance, plan inspection, full holdings, public AI reserves, move suggestions, results, replay and restart confirmation. Support keyboard, mouse and touch, narrow displays, reload resume and interrupted/repeated actions. Preserve unrelated work and servers. Serve loopback only, no paid assets or hosting.

Validate deterministic edge cases and complete matches, browser complete games and interruption flows, independent review and native acceptance. Publish authorized public GitHub repo with README, MIT and full official OS gitignores; commit/push and verify remote SHA and exact-commit CI. Leave durable detached preview.
