# Prism Atelier

A two-artisan competition to build a celestial workshop and earn prestige.

## Language

**Plan**: A development card, held in the market, reserved, or built. A built plan grants one permanent craft discount and its printed prestige.

**Craft**: A permanent discount in one gem color granted by a built plan. It is never spent and is distinct from tokens.

**Patron**: A visitor worth three prestige whose requirements are met by permanent craft. At most one visits per turn.

**Reserve**: A plan held for later purchase, with a maximum of three. Blind reserves are private; market reserves remain public knowledge.

**Exhibition**: The final completed round, triggered when an artisan ends a turn with at least fifteen prestige.
