      THE ARTISAN’S HANDBOOK

      Build an engine of light.

      1. Collect gems. Take 3 different colors. Take fewer only if fewer than 3 colors remain. Or take 2 of one color if at least 4 are in its supply. Gold is never collected this way.
      2. Buy a plan. Pay its cost, reduced by your permanent craft in each color. Each plan adds one permanent discount and may add prestige. Tokens spent go back to the supply. You may use gold for any color, even a color you own.
      3. Reserve a plan. Take a visible plan or a hidden top plan from any tier; also take 1 gold if available. Hold at most 3 reserved plans. They cannot be discarded, only bought on a later turn. Reserving works without gold.
      4. Finish your turn. Return tokens of your choice until you hold 10 or fewer, including gold. Then welcome one eligible patron for 3 prestige. Only permanent discounts qualify; if several qualify, choose one.
      5. Reach the exhibition. At 15 prestige, finish the round so both artisans have equal turns. Highest score wins; a tie favors fewer purchased plans, then a shared victory.

      Four-player supplies: 7 of each color, 5 gold, 5 patrons. Three-player tables use 5 per color and 4 patrons; two-player tables use 4 per color and 3 patrons. Turns follow the displayed clockwise order. Each Automaton knows the public table and its own reserves, never hidden decks or your blind reserves.

      Controls: tap or click any plan to inspect it. Tab and Enter operate every control. Escape cancels dialogs. Press 1–5 to select gem colors, Enter to collect, and H for help when no dialog or form is focused.

      Inspired by the core mechanics of Splendor. Original plan costs, names, and procedural illustrations; no affiliation. Publisher rulebook ↗
